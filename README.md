# RaagSpace — Hindustani Classical Singing Companion

A simple, premium web application built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**, designed for discovering, studying, and singing Indian Classical Raags.

---

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Design Palette
- **Deep Navy Background**: `#060A12` and `#0B1120` with subtle midnight radial lighting.
- **Warm Ivory Text**: `#F6F2EA` and `#FAF8F5` for soft, elegant readability.
- **Subtle Gold Accents**: `#D4AF37` and `#E5C468` for refined hairline borders, highlights, and badges.
- **Typography**: Editorial serif typography (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with crisp modern sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Hero Section**: Elegant typography featuring **RaagSpace**, an evocative tagline about learning and singing raags, and quick access to melodic samples.
- **Choose a Raag Section**:
  - Filter by **Prahar** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night/Midnight) or live search.
  - Curated raags: *Raag Yaman, Raag Bhairav, Raag Bageshri, Raag Darbari Kanada, Raag Bhimpalasi, Raag Malkauns, Raag Jaunpuri, Raag Desh*.
  - Displays Thaat, Vadi, Samvadi, Aroha, and emotional character (*Rasa*).
  - **Hear Aroha**: Built-in Web Audio tone synthesizer plays each swara sequentially with visual note highlighting.
- **Interactive Swara & Raag Modal**:
  - Tap individual swaras (*Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni*) to hear their exact pitch for vocal calibration.
  - Complete Aroha, Avroha, Pakad, and historical/compositional notes.
- **Continuous Tanpura Drone Companion**:
  - Floating acoustic drone in the bottom right corner with root pitch selection (C, C# / Kali 1, D, etc.) to practice singing against a steady drone.
- **Foundations of Riyaz**: 3 minimalist classical principles for singing raags.
